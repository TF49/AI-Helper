macOS says AI Helper “is damaged and can't be opened” — how to fix it

For AI Helper v1.0.88 · macOS 11 and later · Apple Silicon & Intel · Last updated 2026-11-24 · By TF49

Short answer: The download isn't actually damaged. AI Helper is MIT-licensed open-source software that is not notarized by Apple, so macOS Gatekeeper flags anything downloaded from the internet as quarantined and refuses to open it. Open Terminal, run the command below to clear the flag, then double-click the app again:

xattr -cr /Applications/AI\ Helper.app

Messages you might see

  • “AI Helper” is damaged and can't be opened. You should move it to the Trash.
  • “AI Helper” can't be opened because the developer cannot be verified.
  • Apple could not verify “AI Helper” is free of malware.

Option 1: clear the quarantine flag in Terminal (recommended)

  1. Open the DMG you downloaded and drag AI Helper into the Applications folder.
  2. Open Terminal (search for “Terminal” in Launchpad or Spotlight).
  3. Paste and run xattr -cr /Applications/AI\ Helper.app — note the backslash and space between AI and Helper.
  4. Go back to Applications and double-click AI Helper.

xattr -cr recursively removes extended attributes from the app bundle, including the com.apple.quarantine flag. It does not modify the app's code.

Option 2: Control-click and choose Open

Find AI Helper in Finder, Control-click it (or right-click), choose Open, then click Open again in the dialog. If the dialog doesn't offer an Open button, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway.

Did you download the right build?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 to M5 series) need the aarch64 DMG; Macs with an Intel processor need the x64 DMG. You can check your chip under About This Mac.

Only download AI Helper from the official site or GitHub Releases, and never run the command above on apps from sources you don't trust. The official site lists a SHA-256 checksum for every package.

FAQ

Why does macOS say AI Helper is damaged?

The download isn't actually damaged. AI Helper is MIT-licensed open-source software that is not notarized by Apple, so macOS Gatekeeper adds a quarantine flag to apps downloaded from the internet and blocks them. Once you clear the flag, the app works normally.

Is it safe to run xattr -cr?

xattr -cr only recursively removes extended attributes from the app bundle (including the com.apple.quarantine flag); it does not modify the app's code. Only run it on packages downloaded from the AI Helper website or GitHub Releases — the official site lists a SHA-256 checksum for every package.

Is there a way to do this without Terminal?

In Finder, Control-click (or right-click) AI Helper, choose Open and confirm. If there is no Open button, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway.

Which DMG should I download for an Apple Silicon or Intel Mac?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 to M5 series) need the aarch64 DMG; Macs with an Intel processor need the x64 DMG. You can check your chip under About This Mac. AI Helper requires macOS 11 or later.
